Standings

Updated automatically from every finished series.

A series won 2-0 gives the winner 3 points. A series won 2-1 gives the winner 2 points and the loser 1 point. Equal points are split by games won, then head-to-head games, then series won, then goal difference, then goals scored.

SP series played, SW series won, SL series lost, GP games played, GW games won, GL games lost, GF goals for, GA goals against, GD goal difference, Last 5 the five most recent series (W won, L lost).
