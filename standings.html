Standings

Teams are ordered by playoff finish once results exist; regular-season order breaks ties and places teams without a playoff result. Records, points, and form remain regular-season only.

A series won 2-0 gives the winner 3 points. A series won 2-1 gives the winner 2 points and the loser 1 point. Equal points are split by games won, then head-to-head games, then series won, then goal difference, then goals scored.

Playoff finishes are Champions, Finalist, Semi-finalist, Quarter-finalist, or the team's current stage. Playoff series are excluded from regular-season statistics and form. SP series played, SW series won, SL series lost, GP games played, GW games won, GL games lost, GF goals for, GA goals against, GD goal difference, Last 5 the five most recent regular-season series (W won, L lost).
